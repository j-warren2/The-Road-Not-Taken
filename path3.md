Scene 1

You begin your way down the passage. As you move, you glance behind you, and notice there is now a shut door at the end of the hallway. No turning back. \n
There is a light at the end of the tunnel though, literally. It becomes brighter and brighter until you eventually step into a room. There’s vegetation, a sky with a bright sun, and even the sounds of birds chirping. But as you explore, you realize that it’s all artificial. The bark of the trees are smooth and cool. You can hear the faint buzzing of artificial lights coming from the painted sky. The birdsong repeats and repeats. Midway through your exploration, you are startled by a harsh, grating sound in the distance.

A- Follow the sound (CA)
Well, something unpleasant is better than nothing. You start walking cautiously towards the noise, getting louder and louder as you approach. In a fake tree, you spot what is making that accursed noise. A crow. Its beady eyes meet yours, and it changes its tune. \n\n

“Fool, fool!” It declares, puffing out its chest. It caws once more before it flies off.

B- Head the opposite way of the sound (CB)
That did not sound like something nice in the slightest. You rush your way through the fake woods, pushing past plastic leaves and branches, and encounter a wall. The screech doesn’t sound like it’s getting closer, but it isn’t fully gone either. There has to be a way out of this room. But which way would take you there?

You are already midway through, and you aren’t going to stop! You resolutely ignore the screeching sound, keeping up with your detective work. Knocking on every tree, flipping over every stone, leaving nothing unturned. It pays off, though. You eventually discover two trees with strange lines in them, somehow more unnatural than the rest of the fake trees. Pressing against them, they pop open, revealing their hollow cores. One has a ladder down into the depths, the other has a ladder up into the false sky.

A- Run after it (CAA)
You chase after the strange bird as it weaves through the trees. You struggle to keep track of it, but every time you think you accidentally lose it, it somehow appears back into your sight. Maybe it wants you to follow. \n
Even with that ominous thought, you keep racing behind it. The trees start to disappear. The walls begin to close in. And the ceiling keeps getting higher and higher, until you can’t see it anymore. In this long, narrow hallway you find yourself led to, there is eventually a perch that the crow settles down on. \n\n

“Fool, fool!” It repeats, its taunt echoing. You come to a stop below it, and find there are three pedestals in front of you. One contains a crown, enthralling in its splendor. Another contains a jester’s hat. And the last holds a long spear.

A- Choose the crown
B- Choose the jester’s hat
C- Choose the spear
B- Get Revenge (CAB)
How dare a bird call you a fool? You’ll show it! You begin to climb up the tree, until you reach the spot it was perched in. As you suspected, there is a nest with an egg inside! You crush the egg, hollow and fake just like the rest of this place. But as you prepare to climb back down, you notice there are now hundreds of beady eyes, staring right at you. And before you can mutter a single word, they surround you.

A- Go right (CBA)
Right’s always right, right? You follow the wall, clinging closely to it. Eventually, you find a dull, rusted door. You go through it, breathing a sigh of relief as the horrible sound disappears. The door has no handle on this side. \n
This room is dilapidated, to put it lightly. What once might have an artificial garden has withered with time. Exposed wires and broken tubes litter the floor. But as you walk in, you begin to feel woozy. You notice that there are wires all around you, binding your limbs. And connected to those wires, there are glass roses. Filling up with red.

B- Go left (CBB)
Left is lucky, maybe. You follow the wall, clinging closely to it. Eventually, you find a metallic door. You go through it, breathing a sigh of relief as the horrible sound disappears. The door has no handle on this side. \n
And you immediately drop down. Looks like left is not lucky, after all.

A- Take the ladder down (CCA)
You enter the hollow tree and begin your descent. It’s cramped. You can barely slide down, and there is no chance you would be able to maneuver your body to go back up. As you go further and further down, you start to regret your decision. Up is good, isn’t it? Why did you decide to go into a hole? \n
Your panicked pondering is quickly halted as you realize your feet are now dangling. There is no ladder anymore. What should you do?

B- Take the ladder up (CCB)
You enter the hollow tree and begin your ascent. Up is good, you think. There’s plenty of space as you go. In fact, you enter a large chamber, with the ladder fastened to a wall. You feel more and more confident as you climb that this was the right choice. That this was the way you were meant to go. And in another situation, it might have avoided certain death. But not here. You feel the ladder crack under you, and instead of plummeting downwards, you suddenly begin floating. \n
There is nothing here for you. No way for you to grab onto a wall. No way to go back down. And as you float further and further away from the hole that you entered from, you realize your fate. You are trapped, floating in an endless abyss for all of your limited days.

C- Keep exploring. There must be more to find. (CCC)
You begin to obsessively check over the area again. There must be something else! Something better! Something that is clearly the right path. Again and again, you keep looking and looking. The artificial light never dims and time loses all meaning.
There has to be something. There has to. You just have to look again. And again.

A- Choose the crown (CAAA)
The crown glows a strange hue as you pick it up. It’s beautiful, perfect in a way you find impossible to describe. You must put it on. It would make you perfect. As it goes on your head, your vision fades. \n
When you come back to consciousness, you can see your own body far below you. Wearing that crown, lying in a lifeless heap on the ground. And from your own mouth- no, beak- comes one word. \n\n

“Fool!”

B- Choose the jester’s hat (CAAB)
The colorful cap jingles as you pick it up, bells on the end of its peaks. Maybe the crow was telling you a hint. You put on the hat, which is a bit too big for your head. You look up at the crow. It’s looking back, its beady animal eyes very much unreadable. But with a nod of its head, it begins to fly again, further down the hallway. \n
At the end, there is a black and white door. You feel a sudden weight on your shoulder as the crow lands on it. But instead of screeching into your ear, it merely drops a black key in your hand. Seems like you have a buddy now. You unlock the black door and it opens with a slow creak. And warmth, real sunlight, hits both of your faces.

C- Choose the spear (CAAC)
You do not know what intentions this crow has towards you. You woke up on a cold floor, and you’re just supposed to trust the thing that called you a fool? No. You refuse. \n
With as much force as you can muster, you throw the spear up! It hits the crow with a sickening crack. But instead of crying out in pain, it merely laughs as it falls. \n 
There is no blood. Just more wires and gears. Artificial like the rest of this place. But clenched in its claws is a white key. You move down the hallway, spear in hand, until you find two doors. Black and white. You unlock the white door and walk through. \n
Screaming. Maddening, completely maddening! You clutch at your spear, gritting your teeth.
