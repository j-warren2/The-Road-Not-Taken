# THE ROAD NOT TAKEN
## Path 1: Left Passage
### Team Trie-Hards | COP3530 Project 2

---

## Scene 1 [SHARED]

You wake up on the ground. Cold floor, low light, no memory of how you got here. There's a sound somewhere ahead, it's faint... maybe human? The corridor behind you has already sealed shut. You checked. Twice. Three passages open in front of you. Somewhere in this place is a way out... you just have to find it. Pick a road. You're good at that.

### Options

**A:** left [Path 1] <br>
**B:** middle [Path 2] <br>
**C:** right [Path 3] <br>

---

## Scene 2 (Option A, left; Path: A)

The left passage is narrower than it looked. A lot narrower. You turn sideways to fit, which is fine. This is fine. <br>
There's a light ahead. Moving. Someone carrying it, walking away from you. Getting smaller. <br>
You almost call out. <br>
Then you stop. <br>
To the right: a cracked door. Light coming through the gap at the bottom. Not this-place light. <br>
Real light. Outside light. <br>
Your heart does something embarrassing. <br>
The person with the light is almost gone. You have maybe ten seconds. <br>

### Options

**A:** call out to them <br>
**B:** go through the door <br>

---

## Scene 3 (Option A, call out; Path: AA)

They stop. <br>
You know them. Not dramatically. Just... you know them. The kind of person who faded out of your life slowly, the way those things go. You kept choosing other things. Then one day they just weren't there anymore. <br>
_And now they are here._ <br>
They don't look surprised. That's the part that gets you. <br>
"Been in here a while," they say. "Mapped most of it." <br>
They hold up the light. The battery indicator is blinking red. <br>
Every junction in this place splits exactly twice, they say. Not once. Not three times. Always two. Like a system. Like someone designed this on purpose and then put people in it and called it a maze. <br>
They think they found a way out. The passage ahead is new ground. They've been working up to it for a while. <br>
The light flickers. <br>

### Options

**A:** "Let's go. Right now." <br>
**B:** "Wait. How long have you actually been in here?" <br>

---

## Early Ending (Option A, go now; Path: AAA)

You go. <br>
Fast. The passage twists left, then right. You can feel something like hope, which you haven't had since you woke up on that cold floor. That's probably a bad sign. <br>
Dead end. <br>
Solid wall. No marks. No door. Nothing. <br>
You put your hand against it. <br>
It doesn't move. It was never going to. <br>
Behind you, somewhere in the dark, you can still hear them. Still moving. Still looking. <br>
... <br>
... <br>
You chose the road. You just never checked where it went. <br>

**GAME OVER** <br>
*Two roads diverged. You took the one that felt right. That's the thing about roads. They don't care how you feel.*

## Scene 4 (Option B, asked how long; Path: AAB)

They think about it. <br>
"Long enough to know the layout." A pause. "Long enough to stop counting." <br>
You walk beside them. For a while it almost feels normal. Like you're just going somewhere together the way you used to, before you kept choosing other things. <br>
The light flickers. Longer this time. <br>
Every junction in this place splits exactly twice. No exceptions. Someone built this. Someone designed a system, filled it with people, sealed the exits, and left. <br>
The mapped sections end in twenty meters. <br>
"One of us should go first," they say. "Past this point, I don't know what's there." <br>
The light blinks. <br>

### Options

**A:** you go first. You've been waiting long enough. <br>
**B:** let them lead. They know this place. You don't. <br>

---

## Ending (Option A, go first; Path: AABA)

You step ahead. <br>
The passage curves. Then forks. You take left. <br>
Dead end. <br>
Different kind, though. There's writing on the wall. Hundreds of scratches. Names maybe. Dates. Someone was here a long time before you, marking every turn, recording every dead end, leaving something behind just to prove they'd been there. <br>
You find a blank space and scratch in your initials. <br>
Small. But something. <br>
The passage doesn't open. But sitting down against the wall, you don't feel trapped the same way anymore. <br>
You wait. <br>
... <br>
*The road less traveled had no guarantee. But you went anyway. That's the part worth keeping.*

---

## Scene 5 (Option B, let them lead; Path: AABB)

They move fast now. Confident. The mapped sections fall away behind you, one junction at a time. <br>
Then they slow. <br>
One final fork. Two passages. Perfectly symmetrical. No marks. No hints. Nothing to go on at all. <br>
"I've never been past here," they say quietly. "I always turned back." <br>
They look at you. The light is almost gone. <br>
This is the place they couldn't get through alone. <br>

### Options

**A:** left. You have a feeling. You've learned to trust those. <br>
**B:** right. Let them decide this one. You've been guessing long enough. <br>

---

## Ending (Option A, go left; Path: AABBA)

Left. <br>
The passage opens. <br>
Just like that. Like the maze got tired. Like it was always going to end here and was just waiting for you to show up. <br>
There's a door at the end. Real light around every edge. You push it and the air hits you first. Warm. Different. Outside. <br>
You look back. They're still standing at the fork. <br>
You could call to them. You could wait. <br>
You don't know if they'll follow. <br>
The door is open. You found it. <br>
... <br>
*Two roads diverged. You took the one that felt right. This time, for once, it was.*

---

## Ending (Option B, go right; Path: AABBB)

They choose right. <br>
The passage runs long. Longer than it should. It curves back and keeps going and you start to wonder if you made a mistake. <br>
Then it opens into a small chamber. <br>
High on the wall: a window. Way too small to climb through. But sunlight is coming through it. Real sunlight. You can feel the warmth from here. <br>
You both just stand there. <br>
"There's a way out," they say quietly. "We just haven't found it yet." <br>
You nod. <br>
For the first time since you woke up on that cold floor, not yet feels like enough. <br>
... <br>
*You knew, before you'd taken a single step, which road you'd end up on. But the road taught you something anyway.*

---

## Scene 3 (Option B, door alone; Path: AB)

The door opens into a room. <br>
One way in. One way out: a door at the far end, light coming through the gap at the bottom. <br>
Real light. Outside light. It is right there. <br>
You try the handle. <br>
The handle is on the other side. <br>
Of course it is. <br>
You can see it through the gap. It's just sitting there, completely reachable, completely useless to you from this side. You could fit something thin under the door, hook it, pull it toward you. Maybe. Probably. <br>
Behind you, faint, you can still hear the other person. Getting further away. <br>

### Options

**A:** you can get this open. You just need something thin enough. <br>
**B:** go back. You need help with this one. <br>

---

## Scene 4 (Option A, try the door; Path: ABA)

There's a wire bracket on the wall. Loose. You work it free. Thirty seconds of swearing under your breath. <br>
You thread it through the gap. <br>
The handle catches. <br>
The door moves. <br>
One inch. <br>
Two inches. <br>
Something wedged on the other side. But you can feel the air already. Warm. Real. Pouring through that gap like it has been waiting for you specifically. <br>
Two inches of outside. <br>

### Options

**A:** push harder. You are not stopping this close. <br>
**B:** step back. Two inches is not enough. There has to be another way. <br>

---

## Ending (Option A, push harder; Path: ABAA)

You push. <br>
Three inches. Four. Warm air coming through steady now, real and close. <br>
The wire slips. <br>
The handle releases. <br>
The door closes. Not slammed. Quietly. Gently. Like it was never really open for you. Like the whole thing was already decided before you picked up that wire. <br>
You stand there for a while. <br>
... <br>
... <br>
You took the road less traveled. Someone, somewhere, is taking the other one right now. Still hoping. <br>

**GAME OVER**

---

## Ending (Option B, step back; Path: ABAB)

You step back. <br>
Two inches isn't enough. You know the difference between almost and there. <br>
You leave the room. Walk deeper in. Deliberately this time. Not panicking. Just moving. <br>
Left branch. Right branch. Another left. <br>
There's a door at the end of a passage you almost walked past entirely. <br>
It opens from your side. <br>
... <br>
*The road you took because you had no other options turned out to be the one that went somewhere.*

---

## Early Ending (Option B, go back for help; Path: ABB)

You go back. <br>
The light is already smaller. <br>
You pick up the pace. <br>
It gets smaller faster than it should. <br>
You call out. <br>
Nothing. <br>
The light disappears. <br>
You stand there after. Long enough to know it's not coming back. Long enough to understand that knowing doesn't actually change anything. <br>
Eventually you turn and walk back into the dark. <br>
... <br>
... <br>
Two roads diverged. You stood at the fork so long deciding, the road you finally chose had already moved on without you. <br>

**GAME OVER**
